================================================================================
                    NEXUSDRIVE AI — BATTERY RESCUE COPILOT
================================================================================

1. SCREEN OBJECTIVE
Handles critical EV battery situations when remaining charge is insufficient to safely
reach destination or drops below the 20% rescue threshold.

2. RESCUE TRIGGER LOGIC
Battery Rescue is automatically engaged when:
- Current battery percentage < 20.0%, OR
- Projected arrival reserve SoC for the chosen route < 15.0%

3. RESCUE COPILOT WORKFLOW
1. Battery Risk Assessment Card:
   - Current SoC (highlighted in Critical Red).
   - Distance to destination.
   - Deficit analysis (e.g. "Direct arrival reserve is -2.4% -> Stranding Hazard").
2. Local Charger Fallback Discovery & Ranking:
   - Queries local charging dataset offline.
   - Ranks compatible fast chargers using composite score:
     Score = (10 / (Dist + 0.5)) + (PowerKw * 0.1) + (AvailablePorts > 0 ? 5.0 : 0.0)
   - Recommends the highest ranked station (e.g. 60kW DC Hub, 4 ports, 1.8 km away).
3. Route Recalculation via Charger:
   - Constructs a new rescue route with waypoints:
     Current Position -> ⚡ Recommended Station -> Final Destination.
   - Recalculates total travel time including charging duration to 80% SoC.
   - Updates projected arrival reserve to a guaranteed safe buffer (~72%).
4. Decision Explanation:
   - Transparently states: "Route recalculated because current battery (15%) cannot safely
     reach destination without stranding. Diverting to Indiranagar Hub (60 kW DC) provides
     sufficient charging with 4 open ports."
5. One-Tap Action:
   - "Accept Rescue Route & Divert" updates navigation with zero cloud dependency.
